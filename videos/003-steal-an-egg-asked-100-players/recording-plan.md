# Recording Plan: 003 I Asked 100 Steal An Egg Players

**Total: about 5–7 hours of recording over 2–3 sessions.** Record everything. You'll use about 18 minutes of it.

## Before you start
- [ ] OBS set up (see `playbook/recording.md`): 1080p 60fps, game audio and mic on separate tracks
- [ ] **Fresh or low account.** Note your starting speed: `{___}`
- [ ] Make chat **readable**: bigger chat if possible, and screenshot any answer that scrolls too fast
- [ ] A notes doc open: `[time] player # | their speed | trick | WORKS/FAKE | funny?`
- [ ] Mic on. Talk out loud while playing. Live reactions are better than voiceover.

## Session 1: noobs (players 1–30) · about 1.5 hrs
- Join **normal public servers**. Ask low-speed players near spawn.
- Ask everyone. Ignores, trolls and "subscribe to me" all count toward 100.
- Test tricks right away. Keep the ones that work for your own progress.
- **Must get:**
  - [ ] Your avatar **alone at spawn** (promise shot) at the start, before you've done anything
  - [ ] A **funny troll or fail** (cold open #2)
  - [ ] An **ignore montage** (4–5 people ignoring you)
  - [ ] One **surprise noob trick that works**

## Session 2: mid players (players 31–70) · about 2 hrs
- Ask players in the middle of the speed range.
- Do **mini stories** for good tricks: record the failed attempts too, not just the win.
- **Must get:**
  - [ ] Your **best visual trick that works** (cold open #1)
  - [ ] A **mid-video twist** (opposite advice / someone shows you live / first real egg / big troll)
  - [ ] Your **speed number** on screen at the halfway point
  - [ ] Your **first egg you couldn't have stolen at the start**

## Session 3: rich players + finale (players 71–100) · about 2–3 hrs
- Look for servers with high-speed players. Check the leaderboard.
- **Zoom on every rich player's speed** before asking.
- **Must get:**
  - [ ] A **rich player flexing** with a huge speed number (cold open #3)
  - [ ] The **"use all the tricks together"** moment: steal your biggest egg yet
  - [ ] **Server-hopping** to find the #1 player (record every hop, it becomes a montage)
  - [ ] **The leaderboard** with #1 visible
  - [ ] **The ask** to the #1 player: walk up slowly, type it, wait for the answer
  - [ ] **The test** of his trick from more than one angle (do it 2–3 times)
  - [ ] Your **final speed number**

## B-roll (grab anytime, editors always need it)
- [ ] Wide shots of each biome
- [ ] A boss chasing someone (not you)
- [ ] Big eggs spawning
- [ ] Rich players' bases
- [ ] The leaderboard (several times through the day)
- [ ] Night reset happening

## Rules while filming
- Be friendly in chat. Short messages. No personal info, and don't ask for theirs.
- **If someone suggests a WiFi-off / lag / exploit trick:** say "nah, that's how you get banned" out loud, don't do it. Keep the moment, it's good content.
- **Blur usernames in the edit**, especially the #1 player unless they say it's fine.
- If the #1 player's trick flops, **don't fake it.** The script has a backup ending.
