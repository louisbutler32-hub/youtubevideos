## How the WOLF engine fits
- **Question in viewers' heads:** "what's the best trick?", and every new player's answer is a new mini mystery
- **Count:** 100 players, a counter in the corner
- **Ladder:** player SPEED/RICHNESS. Start asking noobs (bad/funny tricks), then mid players, then the richest players in the server. **Finale: the #1 player on the leaderboard's secret.**
- **Promise (say by 0:30):** *"I'm new to Steal an Egg, so I asked 100 players for their best trick. The noobs told me some dumb stuff. But the #1 player on the leaderboard? What he showed me actually worked."*
- **Re-hooks:** "That was the noob tier. Now I'm asking people with a TRILLION speed."

## Filming plan (about 4–6 hours total, no grind)
1. **Setup:** a fresh or low account is fine. Being a noob is the point. Turn on the chat log so it's readable in the recording.
2. **Ask method:** walk up and type something short and friendly: *"I'm new, what's your best trick??"* Screenshot or record their answer.
3. **Test it immediately** on camera. Verdict stamp: WORKS ✅ / FAKE ❌ / "kinda".
4. **Ladder order:** in each server, ask low-speed players first and the richest last (use the in-game leaderboard). Across servers, aim for the highest leaderboard players at the end.
5. **Finale:** find a server with a top leaderboard player (high-speed players are on the server list). Ask them. Test their trick as the big payoff.
6. Players who ignore you, troll you or give terrible advice are **content**. Keep them in tier 1.
7. You don't need 100 good answers. 100 *asks*, with maybe 30–40 tested tricks, is plenty.

## Backup material
If players repeat known tricks, `research/steal-an-egg/myth-bank.md` already tells you what other creators found. Use it to add "this one's been around for ages" commentary.

## Safety/rules
- Keep chat friendly. Don't share personal info, don't ask for theirs. Blur usernames if anyone seems young or uncomfortable.
- No WiFi-off/lag/exploit tricks on camera even if a player suggests one. Say "nah, that's how you get banned" and move on, which is a good moment anyway.
