# Script Outline (easy read)
## I Asked 100 Steal An Egg Players For Their Best Trick

About 18 minutes. Two counters on screen the whole time: **players asked (0/100)** and **your speed**.

---

### 1. Opening: 3 quick clips (0:00–0:18)
No intro. Straight into the action.

1. **A trick that works**
   > "This kid told me if you ___, the boss can't touch you… wait. WAIT."
   ✅ WORKS
2. **A trick that fails (funny)**
   > "This guy said ___. … Thanks bro."
   ❌ FAKE
3. **A rich player showing off**
   > "Then I asked someone with ___ trillion speed. And he showed me this… yo."
   (No answer. Cut away.)

---

### 2. The promise (0:18–0:30)
You alone at spawn. Counter shows **0/100**.
> "I'm new to Steal an Egg. Like, two weeks new.
> So I asked 100 players for their best trick.
> Noobs first. Then people with trillions of speed.
> And the number one player on the leaderboard?
> He told me one that actually worked."

---

### 3. The noobs: players 1–30 (0:30–4:00)
**Fast and funny.** 5–10 seconds each.

Every player is the same pattern:
1. "What's your best trick?"
2. Their answer on screen
3. Test it
4. ✅ / ❌ / 🤷

Things to keep if they happen:
- People ignoring you (make a montage)
- "Subscribe to me" / "give me your egg"
- Someone stealing your egg while you're asking
- 3 people giving the same trick

**Backup tricks to ask about:**
- King Gobble hits for you if you stand close
- Red targets on King Gobble = 2x feathers
- 3 ads = free copper mutation
- Steal right before night

End with **one noob trick that actually works.**

> **Re-hook:** "That's 30 noobs. ___ tricks worked. Next I'm asking people who can actually steal the good eggs."

---

### 4. The mid players: players 31–70 (4:00–10:00)
**Tricks get real.** Good ones get a mini story: try → fail → figure it out → works.

**Backup tricks to ask about:**
- Swap all your bats on King Gobble = 5x damage
- Rejoin a new server mid-event = double feathers
- Heaven fuse zone at night = teleport head start
- **Enchanted Forest back corner = bosses can't reach you** (very visual)
- Ambush a player with a trap and take their egg

**Twist around 7:00** (whichever happens):
- Two players say the opposite thing
- Someone shows you the trick live
- You steal your first egg you couldn't get before
- Someone trolls you hard

> **Halfway line:** "That's 50. Halfway. I'm at ___ speed just from tricks people told me."
> **Re-hook:** "70 players. Now I'm asking people with TRILLIONS of speed."

---

### 5. The rich players: players 71–99 (10:00–15:00)
**Show off their speed every time.**
> "This guy has ___ trillion speed. I have ___. Uh. Hi."

**Big moment around 14:00:** use all the tricks that worked to steal your biggest egg yet.
- **1.25x Turkey Shop speed** (said to make you faster than everyone)
- Enchanted Forest corner
- Server hop for a bigger egg
> "I was a noob an hour ago."

> **Re-hook:** "99 players. One left. The number one player on the leaderboard."

---

### 6. The finale: player #100 (15:00–18:00)
**Slow it down.**
1. Server-hop to find him ("Not here… not here… wait.")
2. Walk up. His speed on screen. "Okay. Be cool."
3. "What's your best trick?" Let the pause sit.
4. Test it. Fail first if it really happened. Then it works.
5. Replay in slow-mo. Biggest ✅ of the video.

**If his trick flops:** don't fake it.
> "The number one player in the game… gave me the worst trick of the whole video."

---

### 7. Ending (under 15 seconds)
> "100 players. ___ tricks that actually work.
> I started with ___ speed. Now I've got ___.
> If you've got a trick nobody told me, comment it. Best one goes in the next video."

---

### Don't do on camera
WiFi-off tricks, scripts, auto-clickers. If someone suggests one:
> "Nah, that's how you get banned."
