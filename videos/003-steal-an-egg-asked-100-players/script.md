## Why it's built like this
- **0:00 starts mid-action.** No "hey guys", no name, no logo.
- **3 payoffs before the promise:** a win, a fail, a flex. That shows the viewer all 3 kinds of moment they'll get.
- **Payoff 3 has no verdict on purpose.** The first open loop.
- **The promise gives the number (100), the ladder (noobs → trillions → #1), and the finale (the leaderboard player)** in under 12 seconds.
- **Last line is a tease, not a summary.** You don't say what the trick is.

⚠️ Only use the final line you can back up. If the #1 player's trick flopped, change the promise to match ("…and what he told me was NOT what I expected").
