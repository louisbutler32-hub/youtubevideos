# Script: 003 I Asked 100 Steal An Egg Players For Their Best Trick

> [brackets] = on screen / editing. Plain text = what you say. `{curly}` = swap in what actually happened.
> Target length: **16–20 minutes.** Pace: fast. If a clip is funny with no talking, don't talk.
> This is a **skeleton**: the structure and the lines that hold it together. The tricks themselves come from your footage. Send Claude your footage notes and it'll fill every `{gap}`.

## The two storylines running through the whole video
1. **The 100 players** (the counter in the corner, 0 → 100)
2. **You getting stronger.** Every trick that WORKS, you keep using. Show your **speed number** in the other corner going up. A noob who starts at `{50}` speed and ends stealing an egg he couldn't touch at the start is the hidden payoff that keeps people watching.

## 3. TIER 1: THE NOOBS (players 1–30) · about 0:30–4:00
**Feel:** fast, funny, chaotic. 5–10 seconds per player. Lots of trolls, lots of FAKE stamps, 2–3 surprise wins.

[Card: **TIER 1: NOOBS** · tier music]

### The rhythm for every player (copy this)
[Walk up]
`{Player #N}`, what's your best trick?
[Their chat answer on screen, big]
[Test it, 3–8 seconds]
[Stamp: WORKS ✅ / FAKE ❌ / KINDA 🤷]
[Optional one-line reaction] `{"Okay that's actually smart." / "Why would you lie to me." / "He just left."}`

### Running gags to look for (keep any that happen)
- Players who **ignore you** → montage of 4–5 in a row, counter flying up. "Okay. Nobody likes me."
- Players who **say "subscribe to me"** or **"give me your egg"** → quick cuts, FAKE stamps
- A player who **steals your egg while you're asking** → GOLD. Keep the whole thing.
- The same trick from **3 different people** → "Okay, why does EVERYONE know this?" Then test it.

### Surprise win (end of the tier, 20–30 sec)

[**WORKS ✅**, speed counter ticks up if it helped you]
