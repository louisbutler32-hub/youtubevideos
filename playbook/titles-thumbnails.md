# Titles & Thumbnails

**Decide these BEFORE recording.** If the idea can't make a great title and thumbnail, it won't get clicked, however good the video is.

## Title rules
- Under about 55 characters so it doesn't get cut off on mobile.
- **Game name in the title.** That's how people searching or browsing for the game find you.
- One big hook: a **number**, a **superlative** (Rarest, Most, Every) or a **time limit**.
- Words that win in this niche (from WOLF's outliers): *Rare, Rarest, Secret, Myths, Anomalies, Insane, 100, 24 Hours, Every, But*.
- Make our own phrasing. Don't reuse a competitor's exact title.

### Title formulas
- Busting [100] Myths In [Game]
- I Tested [Game]'s Most Viral TikTok Hacks
- [Game] Secrets That Are Actually Real
- [Game], But Every [Round] Gets More [Extreme]
- I Spent [24 Hours] Getting The Rarest [Item] In [Game]
- Rarest [Things] In [Game] (That You've Never Seen)
- [100] Players vs [Big Thing] In [Game]

## Thumbnail rules
- **One clear idea.** Readable on a phone at thumbnail size.
- **The most impossible-looking moment** from the video, made bigger and brighter.
- Big, saturated colours. Bump contrast and saturation.
- A character with a strong reaction (your avatar, a posed Roblox character, or a 3D render).
- **3 words max** of text, and **don't repeat the title**. Add to it. (Title: "Busting 100 Myths In Steal An Egg", thumbnail text: "IT'S REAL?!")
- Red circles and arrows on the key thing still work for this audience.
- Make **2–3 versions** and use YouTube's "Test & Compare" (A/B test) on every upload.

## Free tools
- **Canva** or **Photopea** (free Photoshop in the browser)
- In-game screenshots at max graphics, or Roblox Studio to pose characters
- Claude can generate thumbnail concepts and score titles with the connected YouTube tools. Ask "give me 10 title and thumbnail combos for [idea] and score them".
