# Editing Playbook

## Software
- **DaVinci Resolve** (free, pro-level). Best long-term choice.
- **CapCut Desktop** is easier and fast for captions, zooms and sound effects. Fine to start with.
- Free sound effects and music: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay. Avoid copyrighted songs, because they'll get claimed and you won't be paid.

## The retention mindset
Kids' attention is short. **If nothing changes on screen for about 3 seconds, something is wrong.** "Change" can be a cut, zoom, caption, sound effect, meme insert or camera angle.

## Editing pass order
1. **Assembly**: drop in clips in ladder order (quick myths → long myths → finale). Ignore polish.
2. **Cut the dead air**: remove every pause, loading screen and walk to the location (unless it's funny). This is the biggest win. Cut harder than feels natural.
3. **Voiceover**: lay in the VO, cut footage to match it.
4. **Pacing pass**: watch at 1x. Anywhere you feel bored, cut more or add something.
5. **Polish**: zooms, captions, sound effects, music, text pop-ups.
6. **Audio mix**: voice is always loudest. Music quiet (-20 to -25 dB under voice). Check on phone speakers.
7. **Final watch on your phone.** Most viewers are on phones or tablets.

## Toolbox (what WOLF-style edits use)
| Effect | When |
|---|---|
| **Punch-in zoom** (110–130%) | On reactions, results, the important thing on screen |
| **Myth title card** ("MYTH #37: you can trap the owl") | Start of each myth, so viewers know where they are |
| **Verdict stamp**: "TRUE ✅" / "BUSTED ❌" | Every result. Big, with a sound effect. |
| **Counter in the corner** ("37/100") | Whole video. Viewers want to see progress. |
| **Time-skip text** ("3 HOURS LATER") | Long challenges |
| **Source clip** (small picture-in-picture of the TikTok, credited) | When introducing a myth from social media |
| **Freeze frame + circle/arrow** | Point out the tiny thing they'd miss |
| **Sound effects** on every cut-in, verdict and fail | Constantly, but keep them quiet |
| **Captions** on key lines (not necessarily the whole video) | Jokes, verdicts, numbers |
| **Music change** per tier | Signals "new section, things are getting bigger" |

## First-30-seconds checklist
- [ ] First frame is action, not a logo or face
- [ ] At least 2 payoffs before 0:20
- [ ] The promise (number + ladder + finale tease) is said by 0:30
- [ ] Music and sound effects are there from frame 1

## Before export
- [ ] Re-hook at the end of every tier
- [ ] No section longer than about 90 seconds without a new beat
- [ ] Counter and verdict stamps are consistent
- [ ] Ending is under 10 seconds after the final payoff
- [ ] Export: 1080p (or 1440p for better YouTube quality), H.264, 60fps, high bitrate

## Shorts from the long video
- Pick 3–5 single myths with the best payoff.
- 9:16 vertical, crop on the action, captions on everything.
- Start on the claim, end right after the verdict. 20–45 seconds.
- Last line: "I tested 99 more. Full video's on my channel."
