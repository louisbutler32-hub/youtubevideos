# Instructions for Claude in this repo

This repo is a YouTube production HQ. Current channel: Roblox extreme-challenge / myth-busting videos for a creator who is **new to Roblox**. Ideas must be doable by a beginner: no scripting, no advanced building, no huge Robux budget unless stated.

## Writing rules (non-negotiable)
Every script, hook, title and description must sound like a real teenager/young adult talking over gameplay, not like AI. Follow `playbook/script-writing.md`, especially the banned-phrases list. Short sentences. Specific numbers. Real reactions. No "Let's dive in", no "In this video", no "buckle up", no em-dash-heavy prose, no triple-adjective lists.

## Before writing any script
1. Read `playbook/script-writing.md` and `templates/script-template.md`.
2. Ask for (or read) the footage notes: we script **around what actually happened in the recording**.
3. Every idea gets the escalation ladder (easy → insane) and a payoff saved for the end.

## Research
- Use the YouTube research tools (Nextlev, Tube AI, vidIQ) for trending games, outlier videos and transcripts. Prefer transcripts/metadata over the expensive "watch video" tools.
- Save useful findings into `research/` so they aren't lost.
- Never copy another creator's video, title wording or thumbnail. Study the *structure*, then make our own version.

## Files
- New video → `videos/NNN-short-name/` with `brief.md` and `script.md` copied from `templates/`.
- New ideas → append to `ideas/idea-bank.md` with a score.
