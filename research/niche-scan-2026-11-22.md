## Concepts (engine + fast filming)

### 🥇 A. Survive Your Ideas: "I Tested 100 Words From Weakest To Most OP"
- **Question in viewers' heads:** "What's the most OP word?" (the guide videos prove people search this)
- **Ladder:** power. Funny/useless words → decent → crazy → the single most broken word or combo.
- **Count:** 100 words, a counter in the corner, a power rating stamp on each (e.g. "1/10 TRASH" to "10/10 BROKEN").
- **Finale:** the most OP word (or a combo) vs the hardest boss, and/or "can 1 word beat the boss alone?"
- **Promise line:** *"That's 3 of 100 words I tested in Survive Your Ideas. We start with the most useless word in the game, and end with one so broken it beat the final boss in 10 seconds."*
- **Film time:** about 3–5 hours of play. Each test is under a minute.
- **Title options:** "I Tested 100 Words In Survive Your Ideas" / "Survive Your Ideas Words From Weakest To Most OP" / "I Found The Most Broken Word In Survive Your Ideas"
- **Thumbnail:** the chat box with ONE mystery word blurred, a giant thing exploding behind it. Text: "THIS WORD?!"

### 🥈 B. Throw Food for Animals: "Throwing Every Food From Worst To Rarest"
- **Ladder:** rarity (common food → secret/infinite-size food)
- **Questions:** "what does the rarest food do?", "how big can an animal get?"
- **Finale:** the biggest animal possible / the infinite size mutation
- **Watch out:** progression may need grinding. Check how fast you can unlock foods before committing.
- **Bonus:** the genre has clones (Throw a Seed, Throw Keys for Cars), so the same video template works 3 times.

### 🥉 C. 100 Miles Down the River: "Mile 1 vs Mile 100"
- **Ladder:** distance is a built-in ladder, and each 10 miles gets scarier.
- **Twist to beat the copycat playthroughs:** "every 10 miles I make it harder" (no weapons, then no raft upgrades, then solo…). That's the MrBeast escalation.
- **Film time:** 2–4 hours. Only worth it if posted within about a week.

### D. Steal an Egg (backup)
The existing brief in `videos/001-steal-an-egg-illegal-tricks/` still works, but the angle is crowded. Use it for the ice biome drop next weekend if that game still looks hot.
