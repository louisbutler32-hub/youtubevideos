# Survive Your Ideas: 100-Word Ladder

*Built 22 Nov 2026 from transcripts of the top Survive Your Ideas videos (Foltyn, Techys, CaylusBlox, KTG, bunhat, plus the "24 best words" and "top 10 words" guides).*

## How the game works (what you need to know)
- It's a **round-based survival game**. Everyone's dropped on a map, a **roulette picks who types**, and whatever they type comes to life (disasters, weapons, creatures, items). Last player standing wins.
- **One word per turn.** The **two-word game pass** lets you type combos (e.g. "nuke flood").
- Some words have **mastery**: use them enough and they upgrade (Gojo → black hole drag, earthquake → adds magma, apocalypse gets deadlier).
- **Some words are blocked** by a filter ("it doesn't let you say it").
- There's a **boss fight mode** (new update: "The Thinker" boss, Godzilla spawns, etc.).
- You can **spend Robux to type next.**

### ⚠️ Filming problem → solution
In a public server you only type when the roulette picks you, so testing 100 words would take forever. Two fixes:
1. **Private server with bots.** A guide creator did exactly this ("I'm using a private server with bots so I can show you these very quickly"). **Check the private server price on the game page before committing.** This is the fast way.
2. **Public servers + type-next purchases.** Costs Robux per word. Only use it for the finale, where real players' reactions are worth it.

Best combo: **film tiers 1–3 in a private server** (fast), then do the **finale in a public server** with real players (funny reactions and real stakes).

## Filming notes
- Get a **clean shot of the word being typed** (the chat or roulette UI) for every word. That's your "MYTH #" card equivalent.
- Rate each word on screen: **POWER 1/10 → 10/10** (a big stamp with a sound effect).
- **Fails are content.** If a word gets blocked or spawns something dumb, keep it. It's funny.
- **Ask your comments and Discord for words** after video 1. That's a free video 2 ("I Tested YOUR Words").
- Note any word that surprises you (useless-sounding but OP, like umbrella or Minecraft). Those are your best cold-open clips.
