# The WOLF Engine: why the 5M video worked (and how to get it without a month of filming)

The topic (99 Nights myths) isn't why it worked. The **structure** is. Here's what's underneath, and the shortcut for each part.

## The 7 parts of the engine

| # | What he does | Why it works on the viewer | The cheap way to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every item is a question the viewer already has** ("can you trap the owl?") | A tiny itch, scratched every 15–30 seconds. Each answer is a mini payoff. | Pick a game where players are ALREADY asking "does X work / what happens if…". The question is the content. |
| 2 | **A big number + a visible count** (100 myths) | Completion drive. Once you're at 37/100, you want to see 100. | The number only needs to be big. 50 quick items beat 10 slow ones. |
| 3 | **The ladder is announced in the first 30 seconds** ("starting with 5-minute ones, ending with one that took 10 hours") | It tells you the best is at the end, so you stay. | **The ladder doesn't have to be TIME.** It can be anything that gets bigger: weakest → most OP, smallest → biggest, cheapest → rarest, mile 1 → mile 100. |
| 4 | **Cold open = 3 payoffs in 25 seconds** | Proves the video delivers before asking for your time | Free. It's an editing choice. |
| 5 | **Re-hooks name specific things coming up** ("soon: trapping the deer in a tree") | An open loop with a picture in your head | Free. It's a script choice (see `playbook/script-writing.md`). |
| 6 | **Effort signal** ("took a month", "10 hours") | Feels valuable and rare, so you respect it | Only the FINALE needs to be big. Everything else can be fast. Or fake the scale with quantity ("100 words"). |
| 7 | **A game kids are obsessed with this week** | Search + browse traffic is already flowing | Pick games in the first 1–3 weeks of their spike (see the niche scan). |

## The key insight for filming fast
WOLF's ladder was **time**, so it took him a month. **Our ladder can be power, size or rarity.** The viewer gets the same feeling (it keeps getting bigger and the best is last) and we film in an afternoon.

> Same engine, different fuel:
> - WOLF: "Myths from 5 minutes → 10 hours" (slow to film)
> - Us: "Words from weakest → most OP" / "Foods from worst → rarest" / "Mile 1 → Mile 100" (fast to film)

## Checklist: does an idea have the engine?
- [ ] Each item answers a question a player of this game already has
- [ ] 30+ items, with a visible counter
- [ ] A clear "gets bigger" ladder I can say in one sentence
- [ ] A finale that's the biggest, most visual thing, teased by 0:30
- [ ] Each item takes under 5 minutes to film (only the finale can be long)
- [ ] The game is in its spike RIGHT NOW
