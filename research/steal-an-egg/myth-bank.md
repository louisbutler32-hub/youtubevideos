## 2. Game basics you need to know

- You **steal eggs** from biomes and run them home while the biome's **boss** chases you. Get touched and you lose the egg.
- More **speed** lets you steal from harder biomes. You buy speed and treadmills with money from hatched pets.
- **Biomes/bosses mentioned:** Candy/Cherry Blossom (fox/Kitsune, tiger), Titan Temple (Gorilla King), Abyss Ocean (Narwhal), Prehistoric (T-Rex), Cosmic (cosmic skeleton), Angels & Demons, Enchanted Forest, Volcano, Alien, Oni Temple, desert/pyramid.
- **Nighttime** sends everyone back to spawn.
- **Fuse machine** combines pets. **Mutations** (maple, copper, dark matter, thunderous) multiply value.
- **No private servers** in this game.
- **Turkey Rampage:** King Gobble spawns at **:15 and :45 every hour**. Hit it for feathers and spend them in the Turkey Shop (1.25x speed, maple mutation, cornucopia egg, maple moose egg, turkey base skin). There's a golden variant and a 0.02% divine King Gobble.
