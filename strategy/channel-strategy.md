# Channel Strategy

## One-line pitch
**We take a trending Roblox game and push it to the most extreme version possible: 100 myths, 1,000 days, the biggest base, the craziest challenge. Every video escalates to an insane finale.**

## Who it's for
Roblox players aged roughly 9–16 who already play (or watch videos about) the game in the title. They click because:
1. It's about a game they care about **right now**
2. The title promises something they can't easily do or see themselves
3. They want to settle an argument ("is that TikTok fake?")

## Our rules
1. **Trending game + extreme concept.** Both. A great concept on a dead game flops; a dead concept on a hot game is a coin flip.
2. **Be early.** Jump on a game when it's rising, not after 50 channels have covered it. (See "Finding the next game" below.)
3. **Every video has an escalation ladder** that ends in something that took hours or looks impossible.
4. **Show, don't tell.** Every claim gets tested on screen.
5. **Beginner-friendly execution.** Our edge is effort and editing, not skill. Grinding 10 hours AFK is "extreme" and needs zero skill.
6. **Original footage only.** Never re-upload or re-cut other people's videos.

## Our formats (start with #1, add others once it works)

### 1. Myth Busting / "Testing 100 Myths" ⭐ main format
Collect myths from TikTok, Shorts, Discord and comments, then test them all from quickest to longest.
- *Why it suits a beginner:* you learn the game while testing. Being bad is fine because the myth is the content.
- Title shapes: "Busting 100 Myths In [Game]", "I Tested [Game]'s Most Viral TikTok Hacks", "[Game] Myths That Are Actually True"

### 2. "[Game], But Every [Day/Round] Gets More [Extreme]"
Each round, crank one thing up: more players, more danger, more money, scarier.
- Title shapes: "[Game] But Every Night Gets Scarier", "[Game] But I Get 10x Richer Every Minute"
- Needs: a game with rounds or days, and friends or editing tricks to "crank" things up (the escalation can be self-imposed rules).

### 3. "I Spent 100 Days / 24 Hours…" grind challenge
Pure time investment. Record a long grind, cut it to the best 20 minutes.
- Title shapes: "I Spent 24 Hours Getting The Rarest [Item]", "Surviving 1,000 Days In [Game]"

### 4. "Rarest / Secret Things In [Game]"
Hunting rare items, avatars, emotes, badges, glitches. Mix with Format 1.

### 5. Compilations (free bonus content)
Once you have 3–4 videos on one game, cut a "400 Myths In [Game]" 1hr+ compilation. WOLF's did 1.5M.

## Finding the next game (do this every week)
1. Ask Claude: *"What Roblox games are rising this week? Show me small channels getting outlier views on them."*
2. Check Roblox's own Charts page (top trending / up-and-coming).
3. Scroll TikTok/Shorts for Roblox: which game keeps showing up?
4. **Green light** if: the game is rising fast, there are lots of TikToks about it but few long YouTube videos doing our format yet.
5. **Red light** if: 10+ channels already did "100 myths in [game]" this month.

## Upload plan
- **Start:** 1 long video per week (15–25 min) + 3–5 Shorts cut from it.
- Shorts = the best single myth from the long video, with the answer at the end, then "full video on my channel".
- After 8–10 videos, look at which format and which game did best and double down on it, like WOLF did.

## What success looks like
- Video 1–5: learning. Goal is finishing them and getting better each time.
- Video 5–15: one video breaks 2–3x your channel average. Copy that video's structure 5 more times.
- Track every video in its `brief.md` post-mortem: CTR, average view duration, where people dropped off.
