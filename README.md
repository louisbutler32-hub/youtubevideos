# YouTube Videos HQ

Everything for planning, researching, scripting, recording and editing our videos lives here.

**Current channel focus:** Roblox "extreme challenge" videos: MrBeast-style escalation applied to whatever Roblox game is trending right now.

## Folder map

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `strategy/` | Channel direction: who we're for, our formats, what makes us different |
| `research/` | Breakdowns of channels/videos we study (start with `wolf-roblox-breakdown.md`) |
| `playbook/` | How-to guides: scripting, recording, editing, titles and thumbnails |
| `templates/` | Copy these into a new video folder each time |
| `ideas/` | Idea bank, scored and ranked |
| `videos/` | One folder per video: `videos/001-short-name/` with brief, script, notes |

## The workflow for every video

1. **Pick the idea**: from `ideas/idea-bank.md`, score it (see the scoring in that file).
2. **Title + thumbnail FIRST**: before recording. If we can't make a title someone *has* to click, kill the idea. (`playbook/titles-thumbnails.md`)
3. **Brief**: copy `templates/video-brief.md` into `videos/NNN-name/brief.md`.
4. **Research**: collect the myths/challenges/steps from TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Discord and the game's wiki. Log them in the brief.
5. **Record**: follow `playbook/recording.md`. Record way more than you need.
6. **Write the voiceover script AFTER recording**: using `templates/script-template.md` and `playbook/script-writing.md`. We script around the real footage, not the other way round.
7. **Edit**: `playbook/editing.md` checklist.
8. **Publish + review**: fill in the post-mortem at the bottom of the brief 48 hours after upload.
